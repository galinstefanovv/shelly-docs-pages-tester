Time-based triggers let scenes run automatically at specific moments or intervals. There are four types: **Timer**, **Schedule**, **DateTime** and **Solar**. The examples below all use a `bedroomBlind` device to show how each trigger type fits a different timing need.

## Timer Trigger

`setTimerTrigger(minutes, handler)` - Repeats the action every N minutes.

Closes the bedroom blind 30 minutes after the timer starts — useful as a safety reset after manual control.

## Schedule Trigger

`setScheduleTrigger(time, days, handler)` - Weekly schedule based on time and selected weekdays.

`setDateTimeTrigger(date, time, handler)` - One-time trigger at a specific date and time. Date format: `"YYYY-MM-DD"`.

## Solar Trigger

`setSolarTrigger(event, direction, time, handler)` - Triggers based on sunrise or sunset. The event is **sunrise** or **sunset**, the direction is **before** or **after**.

Opens the bedroom blind 30 minutes before sunrise — lets natural light in gradually as the day starts.
