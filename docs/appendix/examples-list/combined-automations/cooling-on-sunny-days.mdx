This script automatically closes the blinds when the living room gets hot and bright, but only if someone is in the room. All three conditions must be met at the same time. The blinds close 30 seconds after the conditions are met, giving a small buffer before they actually move. It's useful for keeping the room cool on sunny days without having to do it manually.
