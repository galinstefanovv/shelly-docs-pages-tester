This script runs a weekday evening routine from 6 PM to 7 AM. During that time window, the living room lights turn on when motion is detected, the AC switches on if it gets too warm and opening the front door triggers both a phone alert and the hallway lights. At 7 AM, if the door is closed, everything shuts off automatically to start the day fresh.
