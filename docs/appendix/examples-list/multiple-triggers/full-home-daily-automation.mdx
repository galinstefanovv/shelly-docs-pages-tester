This script handles several things around the house throughout the day. At night (10 PM to 6 AM), any motion triggers the lights - handy for getting up in the dark without searching for a switch. If a window is opened during that same period, you receive an alert on your phone. The AC maintains the temperature between 20°C and 27°C automatically at any time of day. On weekday mornings at 7 AM, the coffee machine starts and the AC shuts off to help you ease into the day. Then every night at 10 PM, the lights, TV and AC all turn off together, helping you prepare for bed.
