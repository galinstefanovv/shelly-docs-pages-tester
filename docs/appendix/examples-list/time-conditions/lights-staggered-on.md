This script turns on three lights every evening at 7 PM, one after another, with a few seconds between each. The staggered effect makes it feel like someone is naturally moving through the house switching things on, rather than everything lighting up at once. It's great for creating a gradual, welcoming atmosphere as the evening settles in or simulating presence.
