This script adjusts three blinds to specific positions every evening at 8 PM, each one moving at a slightly different time. The first after 3 seconds, the second after 10 seconds and the third after 18 seconds. This automation creates a natural feel rather than everything shifting at once. It's a good way to set the mood for the evening without manually adjusting each blind yourself.
