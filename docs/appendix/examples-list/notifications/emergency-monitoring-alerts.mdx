This script provides basic emergency monitoring by tracking critical conditions such as flooding, gas leaks and low voltage. When any of these events are detected, an immediate notification is sent to the user’s phone. The automation ensures quick awareness of potentially dangerous situations, helping to prevent damage to property and improve overall safety with minimal configuration.
