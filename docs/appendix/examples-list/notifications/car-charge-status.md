This script sends you phone notifications about your car's charging status. When your car starts charging, you get a “Car is currently charging” message confirming the charger is working. Once the battery is full and power consumption drops, you get a “Car is fully charged” notification. Both alerts include a short delay to avoid false alarms from brief power fluctuations, so you only get notified when something meaningful is actually happening.
