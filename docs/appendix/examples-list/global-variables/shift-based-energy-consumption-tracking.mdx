This automation helps track energy consumption across two different work shifts in environments such as manufacturing facilities, restaurants, or similar operations. It collects power usage data separately for each shift and calculates the average consumption. At the end of each day, the results are sent via email, providing a clear overview of energy usage per shift. This makes it easier to monitor efficiency, compare shifts, and keep daily records for further analysis.
