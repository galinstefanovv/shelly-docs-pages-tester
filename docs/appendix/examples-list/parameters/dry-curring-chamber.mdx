This script implements full control over the environment of a dry-curing chamber. Specific humidity and temperature conditions are maintained by controlling several devices. Two fans are used to regulate airflow and humidity levels. Temperature is controlled with an AC unit and a heater when needed. Several schedules and notifications ensure that the process is largely automated.
