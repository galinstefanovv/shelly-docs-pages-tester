This script helps reduce the risk of electric shock during bathing by automatically managing electrical devices based on presence and environmental conditions. When someone enters the bathroom, the boiler is turned off while the lights and ventilation are activated. After leaving, the system restores normal operation with controlled delays. Humidity levels are continuously monitored, and the exhaust fan is used to support dehumidification. The combination of motion detection, door events, and timed actions ensures both safety and comfort with minimal user interaction.
