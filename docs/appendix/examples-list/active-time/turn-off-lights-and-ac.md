sidebar_label: Lights & AC off when door closes

This script helps you avoid leaving the lights or AC running when you head to work. On weekdays, between 6 AM and 11 AM, closing the front door automatically shuts off both of them, but only if they're actually on, so it won't do anything unnecessary. It's a handy automation for rushed mornings when you're not sure if you remembered to switch everything off.
