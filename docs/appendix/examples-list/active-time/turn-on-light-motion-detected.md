This script turns a light on and off based on motion, but only when the room is already dim, so it won't switch on during the day when there's plenty of natural light. It runs every evening from 6 PM to 6 AM, meaning the light follows you around: it comes on when you're there and goes off when you leave. It's great for a hallway, staircase or any spot where you want automatic lighting without it staying on all night.
