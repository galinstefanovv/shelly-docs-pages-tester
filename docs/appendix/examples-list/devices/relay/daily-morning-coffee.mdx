This script starts the coffee machine on weekday mornings at 7 AM and on weekend mornings at 10 AM, but only if you're actually up and moving around. If motion is detected, the machine turns on for 10 minutes and you receive a phone notification. It's a nice way to have your coffee ready when you wake up, without it running pointlessly if you're sleeping in.
