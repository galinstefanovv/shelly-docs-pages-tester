This script automatically controls the humidity in the basement, storage room, and bathroom. In each room, when the humidity reaches 65%, the corresponding fan turns on to reduce moisture in the air.

Once the humidity drops to 50% or lower, the fan turns off. This keeps the humidity within a comfortable range without requiring manual control.
